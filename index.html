      HOW TO PLAY

      • The run: 16 teams, four best-of-7 rounds: Division Semifinal, Division Final, Conference Final, The Final. Lose a series and your run is over. Opponents get tougher every round, and the four-time defending champs are waiting.
      • Home ice follows the 2-2-1-1-1 format. The home team wears white and starts with +1 momentum from the crowd.
      • Game-day prep: before each game pick one boost (morning skate, film session, goalie coach…).
      • Facing elimination? Backs against the wall: +1 Leg on the first beat of every shift. Game 7 adds a once-per-game Moment of Truth card.
      • In a game you play the key shifts (20:00, 12:00 and the final 2:00 of each period). Between them the lines roll on and the game is simulated from ratings and momentum.
      • You attack left → right. Each beat you get 3 Legs and a hand from the skaters on the ice. Unspent Legs carry over to the next beat (up to 2), so sometimes the best play is to wait.
      • Attack (blue/red cards): move the puck up, shoot from their zone. The slot gives the best odds.
      • Defend (green cards): their Next Play happens when you press END BEAT. Cards marked COUNTERS lower those odds, and you'll see the numbers drop. A steal cancels their play outright. Each extra steal attempt in a beat is 15% harder.
      • Penalties give the other team a 2-minute power play (+1 card, better odds). Fights: win +3 momentum, lose −2. Pull the goalie when trailing in the final 2:00.
      • Your deck grows: after a win, pick a new play for your team playbook (or upgrade a card). After a loss, Video Review lets you cut a card or upgrade one. Upgraded cards get a +.
      • Injuries: big hits, blocked shots and fights can hurt your players. A call-up from the farm team dresses in their place until they heal, and the injured player's cards go with him.
      • Fatigue builds with every game (and overtime). At 4+ you lose a Leg on each shift's first beat; at 8+ you lose two. Day Off prep and the rest days between series recover it.
      • Superstitions: win a series and the boys pick up a new superstition, a permanent perk for the rest of the run. Off days sometimes bring an event with a choice to make.
      • Between runs: achievements unlock coaching perks and new superstitions. Win The Cup to unlock Dynasty Levels, each adding a new challenge. Your banners hang in the Hall of Fame.
      • Keys: 1–7 play a card · Space end beat · T timeout · P pull goalie.
